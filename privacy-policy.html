1. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact data: name, email address, phone number
  • Request data: insurance type of interest, message content
  • Technical data: security logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) collected automatically for site security purposes
  • Country of access (from IP address): When you visit the site, our consent worker logs the country your IP address resolves to (for example, "CY") in order to apply the correct cookie consent rules. Your full IP address is not stored.

Important: Please do not send sensitive data (e.g. health details) via the contact form or WhatsApp. If sensitive information is required for your insurance request, a human advisor will guide you through a secure channel.

2. Why We Process Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your quote or appointment requests
  • To contact you regarding your enquiry
  • To maintain site security and prevent abuse
  • For marketing communications (only with your separate, explicit consent)

3. Legal Bases

We rely on the following legal bases under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Consent: When you submit a contact form or subscribe to marketing communications, you provide your consent for the processing of your data for the stated purpose.
  • Pre-contractual measures: Processing necessary to take steps at your request prior to entering into an insurance contract (e.g. preparing a quote).
  • Legitimate interest: Processing necessary for our legitimate interests, such as maintaining site security and improving our services, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights.

4. Who Receives Your Data

Your personal data may be shared with the following recipients:

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages, Microsoft Corporation, USA): Hosts the website itself. Data transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Formspree (Formspree Inc., USA): Processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email. Data transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Microsoft Corporation (Outlook, USA): Receives our contact form emails. Data transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Botpress Technologies Inc. (Canada): Provides the AI chatbot widget. Canada benefits from an EU adequacy decision, so no further safeguard is required.
  • OpenAI (OpenAI L.L.C., USA): Provides the language model that generates the AI chatbot responses. OpenAI is engaged by Botpress as their AI provider; we do not interact with OpenAI directly. Conversation content is processed via OpenAI's API. Data transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default.
  • Make (Celonis SE / Make.com): Routes chatbot enquiries from Botpress to our records. Data may be transferred outside the EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google LLC (Google Workspace / Sheets, USA): Stores chatbot enquiries in our business spreadsheet. Data transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (USA): Hosts our consent-logging service (used to store proof of your cookie consent decisions). Data transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Insurance companies: Where you have requested a quote or made a claim, your data may be shared with insurance companies solely for that purpose, with your knowledge and only for the purpose you have requested.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Requests and quotes: up to 24 months after the last communication
  • Claims-related data: as required by applicable law and regulation
  • Marketing data: until you withdraw your consent or 36 months of inactivity, whichever comes first
  • Chatbot conversations: Botpress retains conversation logs for 90 days by default. After that period they are automatically deleted. We can also delete a specific conversation on request — contact us to exercise your right to erasure.
  • Chatbot lead summaries (Google Sheet): A summary of each chatbot conversation is saved to our business Google Sheet for follow-up. Retained for up to 24 months from the last contact, then deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to restriction: You can ask us to restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interest or for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus — Iasonos 1, 1082 Nicosia — commissioner@dataprotection.gov.cy — www.dataprotection.gov.cy

The chatbot's responses must not be used as a substitute for the terms of the insurance policy, the instructions of the insurance company, or the instructions provided by an authorized representative of our company.

In the event of an accident, damage, emergency, or any claim, the user must follow the procedures provided by their insurance policy, including contacting Roadside Assistance, Accident Care, Police, or any other competent body, where required.

E.A.C. Insurance Agencies & Consultants Ltd is not liable for any action or omission based solely on information provided by the chatbot.

Conversations may be recorded and analyzed for quality, training, security, and improvement of our services.

Users must not submit medical information, claim documentation, financial account details, identity documents, passport information or any special category personal data through the chatbot.

No insurance cover, amendment, renewal, cancellation, claim notification or acceptance of risk shall be deemed effective unless confirmed in writing by an authorised representative of E.A.C. Insurance Agencies & Consultants Ltd.
