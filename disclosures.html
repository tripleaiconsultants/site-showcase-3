1. Status

E.A.C. Insurance Agencies & Consultants Ltd is an insurance intermediary registered under the Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD), Registration No. 5149. You may verify our registration via the official register of the Insurance Companies Control Service (ICCS) of Cyprus.

2. Advice

Yes — we provide insurance distribution advice based on a fair and personal analysis of the market, where applicable. We help you identify the right insurance solution based on your needs and priorities.

3. Nature of Services

We provide advice on insurance intermediation. We assist you in identifying suitable insurance products from our selected insurance partners and present options based on your individual requirements.

4. Remuneration

In relation to the insurance contract, we may receive remuneration in the following forms:

  • Commission included in the insurance premium, paid by the insurance company
  • Fee payable directly by the customer (if applicable)
  • Other economic benefit (if applicable)

Where a fee is payable by you, we will inform you of the exact amount before the conclusion of the insurance contract.

5. Complaints Procedure

If you wish to file a complaint regarding our services, you may do so by contacting us at e.a.c.insurances@outlook.com or by telephone at +357 96 660 113.

Our complaints handling process is as follows:

  • We will register your complaint within 3 business days of receipt and send you a written acknowledgment.
  • We aim to provide an initial response within 15 business days.
  • The maximum resolution period is 30 business days from the date of registration. If additional time is required, we will inform you in writing of the reasons for the delay.
  • All complaints and related correspondence are retained for a minimum of 5 years.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman of Cyprus:

  • Address: 13 Lordou Vyronos Avenue, 1096 Nicosia, Cyprus
  • Phone: +357 22 848 900
  • Email: complaints@financialombudsman.gov.cy
  • Website: www.financialombudsman.gov.cy

Filing a complaint does not affect your right to pursue legal action.

Notice: Telephone calls may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance and training purposes.
